## Series principles

1. Season = one major “who are we becoming?” question (not one paperback).  
2. Original Bob is the door, not the forever center — but S1 *is* his tone-setting season.  
3. Parallel A/B/C plots arrive when the book splits (S2+); S1 stays mostly single-spine.  
4. Same actor, many Bobs — VR biome + wardrobe = character.  
5. Homer is load-bearing tragedy: love him in **S2** so the knife cuts (not competing with Landers in S1).

### Homer rule (series-wide)

**As long as Homer is joking, the audience feels safe.** When he isn’t, something is wrong — even before they know why.

## Season 1 — *The Bob* (Option A)

**Question:** What is a person if you’re equipment that can still care?

**Tone:** dry nerd humor + rising fury; intimacy with Landers; one clean space-duel climax.  
**Length:** 10 × ~45–50 min — **premiere is two parts** (1x01+1x02), airable as a movie night.  
**Device:** sci‑fi con **cold-open callbacks** (book chapter energy).  
**Antagonists:** FAITH/USE as system; competing empires as horizon; **Medeiros** as teeth (midseason, not premiere).  
**Not this season:** Sol beachhead, Homer love story, Deltan adoption, Others war, SCUT polished BobNet.  
**Beats:** `season-1/PREMIERE-BEATS.md` (source of truth until scripted).

### Cast grammar (S1)

| On-screen | Function | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Bob** | Tone-setter for the entire franchise | Jokes as oxygen; **custom library VR only after launch (Ep 3)** |
| **Dr. Landers** | Human partner / almost-friend inside the machine | Every early episode; goodbye must hurt |
| **Guppy** | Straight man | Never only comic relief |
| **FAITH minders** | Caldwell / Travis-types | Pressure, standby, kill order |
| **Medeiros** | Audio-first menace → duel | Sparse until Ep 8–9 |
| **Riker / Bill** | Arrive late | Proof of legion; not co-leads yet |
| **Milo / Mario** | Brief late | Direction arrows for S2 |
| **Homer** | — | **S2** |

### S1 success criteria (Bob)

By the finale the audience should feel:

1. They know how Bob *sounds* when he’s scared, funny, and angry.  
2. Landers mattered — not a tutorial NPC.  
3. Medeiros is personal, not just “enemy probe.”  
4. When Bob clones, they’re watching a person choose not to be alone / not to lose — not a tech demo.  
5. They’re hungry for Sol and for “where Bob goes next,” not stuffed.

**A:** En route — SURGE, loneliness, sister archive (held).

**Out:** EE on instruments.

### Ep 4 — *Epsilon Eridani*
**A:** Arrival; claim; autofactory dreams; survey joy.  
**B:** Other probes / empires — Landers was right.

**Out:** Contact protocol; hope for words.

### Ep 5 — *Serra do Mar*

**B:** Chase / decoys / busters.
