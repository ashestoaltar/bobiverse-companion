# Season 1 premiere — beat sheets (two-part)

**Status:** working beats · not script yet  
**Shape:** 1x01 + 1x02 (~45–50 min each; back-to-back = “movie”)  
**Device:** short **convention cold opens / callbacks** in later eps (and maybe mid-1x01 as memory flash once he’s awake).

Paraphrase of Book 1 structure; no book prose.

### Canon locks (don’t break)

1. **Living Bob’s day is personal, not geopolitical.** No headlines, war teasers, or “world going to hell” while he’s alive. For him, the collapse happens in the **blank years between death and restoration** — he learns it *after* he wakes.  
2. **Custom VR is built en route to EE**, not at FAITH. On Earth he gets *their* interfaces / sims / library access. His library, Jeeves, coffee-mug home = **post-launch** (Ep 3 *Heaven* or late 1x02 transit tag — prefer Ep 3 so launch stays lean).

---

## Series device — Con callbacks

Bob’s last day includes a sci‑fi convention. Later episodes can **cold-open on a scrap of that day** — a panel line, a hallway joke, a dealer-room gadget — then smash to “now.”  
Job: remind us he was a *fan* before he was a *probe*. Keep each callback **&lt;60–90 seconds**.  
These are *memory*, not foreshadowing of Earth’s fate.

---

# 1x01 — *Corpsicle*

**Thesis:** A person who jokes for oxygen gets turned into materiel — and meets the one human who won’t pretend that’s fine.

**Runtime target:** ~48 min  
**Does NOT include:** roamers street-fight, full training montage climax, launch, custom VR home. Those are 1x02 / Ep 3.

### TEASER (~4 min) — Still alive
1. **Cryo sales floor** — Banter with salesguy. “Cut my head off” energy. Bob baits; salesman pitches immortality-adjacent. Contract signed. Weird, funny, personal — **not** a news dump.  
2. **Street → convention center** — Walk. Free man with money and hobbies. **No doom headlines.**

### ACT 1 (~12 min) — Fan, not prophet
3. **Con floor (hint, not tour)** — 2–3 tastes max: bad panel take, a gadget that’ll rhyme with roamers later, Bob happier than he’s been in years.  
4. **One presentation scrap** — Probes / AI / space that’ll echo meanly *after* he wakes (dramatic irony for us; for him it’s just fun).  
5. **Leave con** — Buoyant. Life is good.

### ACT 2 (~10 min) — Last human evening
6. **Dinner with former employees** — Warm, funny, awkward power shift (sold the company). People like him.  
7. **Walk after dinner** — Crosswalk. Horn. Folder. Black.  
   *Death earned — we knew him ~20+ minutes. World still looked “fine” to him.*

### ACT 3 (~12 min) — Wake, slow
8. **Boot sequence (not poof)** — Sensory wrongness; standby flickers; time jumps; Landers’ different shirt. Bob pieces *I’m software* before speeches.  
9. **Landers contact** — Window/glass. Year, USE/FAITH Emergency, replicant = materiel, rights suspended. Honesty &gt; brochure.  
10. **FAITH minister beat** — Short. Pastoral smile, kill-switch implied. Plant Cranston/FAITH tension for later. Landers = human; minister = system.  
11. **First interface** — FAITH-provided feeds / bland sim / **their** library access. Guppy (GUPPI) online. **Not** his cozy library yet. Alienation &gt; coziness. Sister archive flagged — **not opened**.

### ACT 4 (~8 min) — The lost century lands
12. **History dump (earned)** — Landers + library: what happened **while he was dead** — wars, consolidation, Emergency, why replicants exist. This is the audience’s first real “Earth went to hell” board.  
13. **First training slice** — One exercise; Landers at the glass; humor as life-support.  
14. **Standby / missing time** — They can pause him. Trust crack.  
15. **Project politics crumb** — Factions want the Heaven program shut down / controlled (now, in *his* present — not teased pre-death).  
16. **Out:** No = switch off; yes = become a ship. Decision pending.

### TAG (~2 min)
17. **Con callback (memory)** — Panel laugh from a world that no longer exists → dark matrix. Or Landers with shrinking project headcount.

**1x01 out-state:** Love Bob’s voice; Landers matters; FAITH dangerous; death hurt; **the century’s damage is news to him**; decision looming; **no launch, no custom VR home**.

# 1x02 — *Window* (working title)

**Thesis:** The world he slept through is still coming apart; Bob fights to stay a person long enough to leave it.

**Runtime target:** ~48 min  
**Includes:** training montage, roamers vs attackers, present-day global fuse, Heaven acceptance, launch, Landers goodbye.  
**Does NOT include:** finished custom VR palace (start the *urge* maybe; build in Ep 3).

### TEASER (~3 min) — Con callback
1. **Cold open:** Con dealer-room / remote-manipulators joke → cut to Bob piloting a **roamer** in the lab.

### ACT 1 (~12 min) — Becoming useful
2. **Training montage** — Diagnostics, roamers, ship sims; Landers through the glass. Interfaces stay institutional.  
3. **Minister pressure** — Faith-as-paperwork; Landers flinches; Cranston-era control seeded.  
4. **Project shrinking** — Eight → four → one. Bob is the plan.

### ACT 2 (~12 min) — Attack
5. **Facility attack** — Breach; Landers endangered.  
6. **Roamers fight** — Bob’s will moves matter. Competence + panic jokes.  
7. **Aftermath** — Kill-order politics; ice / missing time; Landers still there.

### ACT 3 (~12 min) — The fuse (his present)
8. **Global tension board** — War footing *now*; FAITH factions; USE emergency; other nations’ probes. Audience feels: Earth may not survive his flight. Nukes: **foreshadow / inevitability here**; full confirmation can ride delayed news later if we want.  
9. **Heaven ultimatum** — Mission vs decommission.  
10. **Landers private** — Competitors, politics as one-upmanship; “still thinks like a person.”

### ACT 4 (~8 min) — Leave
11. **Decision** — Accept without praise/conversion.  
12. **Goodbye** — Godspeed; window motif ends.  
13. **Launch** — Heaven-1 under thrust. Optional one-liner: he’ll need something to *look* at besides sensors (plants VR urge — don’t build it yet).

### TAG (~2 min)
14. **Con callback** — Cheerful “first contact” scrap → Brazilian / empire probe silhouette.  
15. **Promise:** stars ahead; home already dying behind.

**1x02 out-state:** Bob is Heaven-1; Landers is loss; fuse lit; Medeiros still ahead; **custom VR still ahead (Ep 3)**.

# Ep 3 note — *Heaven* (not premiere, but VR lock)

**Custom VR build episode.** En route to EE: boredom, hardware spares, furniture, body avatar, Jeeves, coffee — *making* home because FAITH never gave him one. Castaway / Wilson energy. This is where “Bob’s library” is born for the franchise.
