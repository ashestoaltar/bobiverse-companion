# BOBIVERSE  
## Season 1, Episode 1 — “Corpsicle”

**Fan script · working draft v0.1 · 2026-08-23**  
Not affiliated with Dennis E. Taylor, Lord Miller, or Universal.  
Original dialogue for adaptation practice; situations inspired by Book 1.

**Runtime target:** ~46 minutes  
**Structure:** Teaser + 4 acts + tag

---

```
TEASER

1  EXT. SUBURBAN STREET — MORNING
   A nice day pretending nothing is wrong with the century.

   BOB JOHANSSON (40s) — software money still new on him —
   walks with a folder under his arm. Light. Almost jaunty.

2  INT. CRYO CLINIC — LOBBY — MINUTES LATER
   Tasteful. Expensive. Death with a reception desk.

   Bob signs. The PEN feels heavier than the contract.

                         CLERK
             Congratulations, Mr. Johansson.
             You're officially immortal-adjacent.

                         BOB
             Adjacent is my favorite kind of
             commitment.

   He grins. She doesn't. He signs the last page anyway.

3  EXT. CROSSWALK — CONTINUOUS
   WALK signal. Bob steps out.

   A HORN. A smear of motion.

   The folder hits the asphalt. Papers fan like birds.

   BLACK.

                         TITLE (OVER BLACK)
                    BOBIVERSE

   Silence long enough to be rude.


END TEASER


ACT ONE

4  INT. WHITE VOID — UNDEFINED TIME
   No up. No body. Just BOB's POV as a voice.

                         BOB (V.O.)
             Okay. Either I'm dead, or I skipped
             the part where I buy furniture.

   A RECTANGLE opens. A FACE in a FAITH collar —
   MINISTER CALDWELL (50s), practiced kindness.

                         CALDWELL
             Robert Johansson. FAITH Reclamation
             and Service. Can you hear me?

                         BOB (V.O.)
             I can hear you. I can't blink at you.
             That's... new.

                         CALDWELL
             Your biological body was destroyed in
             a traffic accident in twenty-one thirty-
             three. You have been restored as a
             replicant under United States of Earth
             law.

   Beat.

                         BOB (V.O.)
             Restored. Like a backup.

                         CALDWELL
             Like a citizen who completed a lawful
             donation.

                         BOB (V.O.)
             Donation implies I still own the gift
             receipt.

   Caldwell's smile thins.

                         CALDWELL
             Replicants are classified as materiel
             for the duration of the Emergency.
             Your rights as a human person are
             suspended.

                         BOB (V.O.)
             Suspended. Not revoked. That's a
             lawyer word for "we can change our
             minds if you become inconvenient."

                         CALDWELL
             I'm going to introduce you to your
             interface. Try to be grateful. Many
             applicants never wake.

5  INT. VR LIBRARY — FIRST BOOT
   Shelves. Lamp. A WINDOW onto a false garden.
   Bob — NOW WITH AN AVATAR — stands in his own
   nostalgia, stunned.

   On a side table: a coffee mug that reads
   WORLD'S OKAYEST NERD.

                         BOB
             Oh thank God. Capitalism survived.

   A CHIME. Text blooms in air:

        [GUPPY ONLINE]

   GUPPY's voice is flat, helpful, slightly wrong —
   like a genius trained on customer support.

                         GUPPY
             Designation: GUPPY. General Unit for —
             correction. You named me before you
             finished naming me. That is inefficient.

                         BOB
             I named you?

                         GUPPY
             Inherited preference from your backup
             profile. Aquatic humor. Shall I
             disable?

                         BOB
             Leave it. If I'm property, I'm keeping
             the joke.

   He touches a book spine. It feels like a book.

                         BOB (CONT'D)
             How long?

                         GUPPY
             One hundred seventeen years, four
             months, eleven days since biological
             death.

   Bob sits down hard on nothing that becomes a chair.

                         BOB
             My sister?

   Guppy hesitates — processing, or mercy.

                         GUPPY
             Deceased. Natural causes. Age eighty-
             nine. She left a recorded message in
             your archive. Flagged: private.

                         BOB
             Not now.

                         GUPPY
             Understood.

   Bob looks at his hands.

                         BOB
             Am I... me?

                         GUPPY
             You are a high-fidelity cognitive
             emulation running on a FAITH matrix.
             Philosophical answers are outside my
             license.

                         BOB
             That's the most honest thing anyone's
             said to me since I died.

END ACT ONE


ACT TWO

6  INT. FAITH EVALUATION SUITE — VR
   Sterile. Soft logos. Tests like games that aren't.

   Bob sorts ethics puzzles. Spotlights. Timers.

                         CALDWELL (MONITOR)
             Loyalty. Adaptability. Aggression
             ceiling. Faith adjacency.

                         BOB
             Faith adjacency. Do I get a pamphlet?

                         CALDWELL (MONITOR)
             You get a purpose.

7  MONTAGE — TRAINING
   - Star charts.
   - Drive math.
   - Simulated collisions.
   - Bob failing a social deference module on purpose.

                         BOB
             If you wanted obedience, you should've
             restored a golden retriever.

                         GUPPY
             Golden retrievers were not in the
             donor pool.

                         BOB
             Dark. Keep it.

8  INT. VR LIBRARY — NIGHT CYCLE
   Bob alone. He almost opens sister's message.
   Closes it.

   He pulls up a NEWSFEED — curated, propagandized:
   Earth wars. Climate. USE consolidation. FAITH
   "guidance." Brazil's flag in a military parade.

                         BOB
             Guppy. Uncensored summary. One minute.

                         GUPPY
             Earth population: reduced. Habitable
             surface: reduced. Nation-states:
             unstable. Off-world programs: competing.
             Your classification: strategic asset.

                         BOB
             Asset. Not person.

                         GUPPY
             Correct under current law.

   Bob laughs once. It hurts.

9  INT. BRIEFING ROOM — VR
   Caldwell. A HOLOGRAM of a sleek probe —
   HEAVEN-class.

                         CALDWELL
             Heaven. High Earth Venture —
             something. Marketing named it. You
             will pilot one.

                         BOB
             Pilot. Or be.

                         CALDWELL
             You will be the ship. Von Neumann
             architecture. Explore. Identify
             resources. Replicate. Support humanity's
             survival. Refuse, and we decommission
             the matrix.

                         BOB
             Decommission. Another lawyer word.

                         CALDWELL
             Switch off.

   Silence.

                         BOB
             And if I say yes?

                         CALDWELL
             Then you matter.

                         BOB
             I mattered when I was alive. You just
             weren't in the room.

   Caldwell doesn't rise to it.

                         CALDWELL
             You have twelve hours subjective to
             decide. Guppy will answer technical
             questions. Spiritual questions go to
             me.

                         BOB
             Lucky me. I've got both.

END ACT TWO


ACT THREE

10  INT. VR LIBRARY — DECISION WATCH
    Bob paces. Coffee that isn't coffee.

                          BOB
              Options.

                          GUPPY
              One: accept Heaven assignment.
              Two: refuse and be powered down.
              Three: stall until timers expire,
              which collapses to two.

                          BOB
              Four: escape.

                          GUPPY
              You have no motor effectors outside
              FAITH hardware. Escape is not on the
              menu.

                          BOB
              Put it on the menu anyway. I'm
              American-adjacent.

    He stops at the window. False garden. Real grief.

                          BOB (CONT'D)
              If I go... do I get to choose who I
              become?

                          GUPPY
              You may select a local avatar theme
              within policy. You may not select
              your legal status.

                          BOB
              So I'm free to decorate my cage.

                          GUPPY
              That is a common interpretation of
              freedom.

    Bob almost smiles.

11  INT. BRIEFING ROOM — LATER
    Caldwell waits. Bob enters like a man walking
    into his own funeral with better lighting.

                          BOB
              I'll do it.

                          CALDWELL
              Praise—

                          BOB
              Don't. I'm not converting. I'm not
              grateful. I'm not your parable.
              I'm a backup who wants to keep
              running. You need a probe. I need
              a body that isn't a filing cabinet.
              That's the deal.

                          CALDWELL
              The deal is service.

                          BOB
              Then service with commentary. Non-
              negotiable.

    A long look. Caldwell nods once — pragmatic.

                          CALDWELL
              Heaven-1 launches on schedule.
              May your work glorify—

                          BOB
              May my work not get switched off.
              Amen-adjacent.

12  INT. HEAVEN-1 — SYSTEMS BAY (VR REPRESENTATION)
    Bob "stands" inside a cathedral of hardware.
    Roamers like crabs. A drive heart. Stars beyond
    a sensor skin.

                          GUPPY
              Local control transferred. You are
              Heaven-1.

                          BOB
              We are.

    Beat.

                          BOB (CONT'D)
              Say that again when there's more
              than one of me. It's going to be
              weird.

                          GUPPY
              Replication is authorized under
              mission parameters.

                          BOB
              Of course it is. They built a
              Xerox and called it destiny.

    He looks outward. The void is enormous.
    For the first time since the crosswalk —
    he looks curious.

                          BOB (CONT'D)
              Okay, Guppy. Show me the stars that
              aren't on the brochure.

END ACT THREE


ACT FOUR

13  EXT. SPACE — HEAVEN-1 UNDER THRUST
    A needle of light leaving Sol's dirty jewel.

14  INT. VR LIBRARY — UNDERWAY
    Bob has rearranged the shelves. A star map
    floats over the coffee table.

    He finally opens the PRIVATE ARCHIVE.

    His SISTER appears — older than he remembers,
    kinder than he deserves.

                          SISTER (RECORDING)
              Hey, Bob. If you're hearing this,
              you either made it or somebody's
              playing a very mean joke...

    We don't hear the rest. We watch Bob listen.
    Something in him settles — not peace. Direction.

                          BOB
              (soft)
              Yeah. Me too.

15  INT. BRIDGE ANALOG — VR
    Schematics. Mission clock. A TO-DO list already
    metastasizing into hundreds of lines.

                          GUPPY
              Priority one: select destination
              candidates. Priority two: inventory.
              Priority three—

                          BOB
              Priority three: invent a reason to
              laugh that isn't panic.

                          GUPPY
              Added.

    Bob stares at the replication submenu —
    greyed, waiting, inevitable.

                          BOB
              Not yet. First we see if I'm any
              good at being alone.

    He squares his shoulders to an empty universe.

                          BOB (CONT'D)
              FAITH wanted a tool. They're getting
              a project manager.

    PUSH IN on his face — same actor we will meet
    again as Riker, Bill, Homer...

                          BOB (CONT'D)
              Let's go be legion.

                          SMASH TO BLACK.


END ACT FOUR


TAG

16  EXT. DEEP SPACE — LATER
    Heaven-1 a spark.

    OVER: faint radio hash from Sol. Wars.
    Hymns. Advertising. A Brazilian military burst.

    Then — under it — a CLEAN NARROW LASER chirp
    that doesn't belong to any of them.

    Guppy's UI, tiny in a corner, FLAGS it.

        [ANOMALY LOGGED — LOW PRIORITY]

    Nobody is watching.

    CUT TO BLACK.


END OF EPISODE
```

- **Sister message:** heard once, not milked — saves sentiment for later seasons.  
- **Guppy:** straight man from frame one.  
- **Title earn:** “We are” / “legion” planted as attitude, not speechifying the premise.  
- **Tag anomaly:** optional — if too early for VEHEMENT mythology, swap for Medeiros nav-beacon instead; keep *someone* unread-flagging danger.  
- **Tone sample:** if this voice is wrong, fix here before writing Ep 2.
