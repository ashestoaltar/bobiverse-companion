# BOBIVERSE  
## Season 1, Episode 1 — “Corpsicle”

**Fan script · draft v0.2 · 2026-08-23**  
Not affiliated with Dennis E. Taylor, Lord Miller, or Universal.  
Original dialogue; situations inspired by Book 1.  
**Supersedes:** `v0.1.md` (archived).

**Runtime target:** ~46 minutes  
**Structure:** Teaser + 4 acts + tag  
**Option A job:** Landers is load-bearing. FAITH is the cage. Bob’s voice sets the franchise.

---

```
TEASER

1  EXT. SUBURBAN STREET — MORNING
   A nice day pretending the century is fine.

   BOB JOHANSSON (40s) — new money, old jokes — walks with a
   folder. Almost jaunty.

2  INT. CRYO CLINIC — LOBBY — MINUTES LATER
   Death with good lighting and a reception desk.

   Bob signs. The PEN feels heavier than the contract.

                         CLERK
             Congratulations, Mr. Johansson.
             You're officially immortal-adjacent.

                         BOB
             Adjacent is my favorite kind of
             commitment.

   He grins. She doesn't. He signs anyway.

3  EXT. CROSSWALK — CONTINUOUS
   WALK signal. Bob steps out.

   A HORN. A smear of motion.

   The folder hits asphalt. Papers fan like birds.

   BLACK.

                         TITLE (OVER BLACK)
                    BOBIVERSE

   Silence long enough to be rude.


END TEASER


ACT ONE

4  INT. WHITE VOID — UNDEFINED TIME
   No body. No up. Bob's voice in a blank.

                         BOB (V.O.)
             Okay. Either I'm dead, or I skipped
             the part where I buy furniture.

   A WINDOW opens — not a face in a collar first.
   A MAN at a desk with a tablet: DR. LANDERS (50s),
   clerical FAITH shirt, tired eyes that still try.

                         LANDERS
             Robert Johansson. Can you hear me?

                         BOB (V.O.)
             I can hear you. I can't blink at you.
             That's... new.

                         LANDERS
             I'm Dr. Landers. I'm going to answer
             what I can, and I'm going to help
             prepare you for what comes next.

                         BOB (V.O.)
             "What comes next" is doing a lot of
             work in that sentence.

   Landers almost smiles. Almost.

                         LANDERS
             What's the last thing you remember?

                         BOB (V.O.)
             Crosswalk. Horn. Folder. Am I —
             wait. Am I a head in a jar? Be
             honest. I've seen the movies.

                         LANDERS
             You're a replicant running on a FAITH
             matrix. Your biological body was
             destroyed in twenty-one thirty-three.
             You've been restored under United
             States of Earth law.

                         BOB (V.O.)
             Restored. Like a backup.

                         LANDERS
             Like a donor who completed a contract.

                         BOB (V.O.)
             Donation usually leaves you holding
             the receipt.

   Landers looks at the tablet — not cruel. Caught.

                         LANDERS
             Replicants are classified as materiel
             for the duration of the Emergency.
             Your rights as a human person are
             suspended.

                         BOB (V.O.)
             Suspended. Lawyer word for "we can
             change our minds if you become
             inconvenient."

                         LANDERS
             I'm not going to lie to you and say
             that's wrong.

   Beat. Bob hadn't expected honesty.

                         BOB (V.O.)
             Okay. You're either the good one, or
             the one they send when the good one
             called in sick.

                         LANDERS
             I'm the one they trained to talk to
             people from your century. Including
             the sarcasm.

                         BOB (V.O.)
             Lucky me. Do I get a body, or are we
             doing the whole season as voiceover?

                         LANDERS
             Interface first. Try not to panic when
             the room appears. Most people do.

5  INT. VR LIBRARY — FIRST BOOT
   Shelves. Lamp. A FALSE GARDEN window.
   Bob — AVATAR ON — stands in his own nostalgia.

   Mug on the table: WORLD'S OKAYEST NERD.

                         BOB
             Oh thank God. Capitalism survived.

   CHIME.

        [GUPPY ONLINE]

                         GUPPY
             Designation: GUPPY. You named me in
             your preference profile before you
             finished naming me. Inefficient.

                         BOB
             I named you?

                         GUPPY
             Inherited humor flag. Aquatic. Shall
             I disable?

                         BOB
             Leave it. If I'm property, I'm keeping
             the joke.

   Landers appears in a side WINDOW — glass between
   them even in VR. Habit. Protocol. Soft prison.

                         LANDERS
             How do you feel?

                         BOB
             Like I died on a Tuesday and woke up
             in a metaphor. How long?

                         GUPPY
             One hundred seventeen years, four
             months, eleven days since biological
             death.

   Bob sits. The chair arrives late, like manners.

                         BOB
             My sister?

   Guppy hesitates — processing or mercy.

                         GUPPY
             Deceased. Natural causes. Age eighty-
             nine. Private archive message flagged.

                         BOB
             Not now.

   He looks at Landers through the window.

                         BOB (CONT'D)
             You're still here.

                         LANDERS
             Standard procedure. We don't leave
             new restorations alone for the first
             hour. People invent worse stories than
             the truth.

                         BOB
             And the truth is...?

                         LANDERS
             You're going to be asked to become a
             ship. You can say no. Saying no ends
             the matrix.

                         BOB
             Switch off.

                         LANDERS
             Yes.

                         BOB
             You say that like you've said it
             before.

                         LANDERS
             I have.

   Not a threat. A scar.

                         BOB
             Then stay in the window, doc. If I'm
             inventing stories, I want a co-author
             who flinches.


END ACT ONE


ACT TWO

6  INT. FAITH EVALUATION SUITE — VR — DAY CYCLE
   Soft logos. Hard timers. Ethics puzzles as games.

   Landers at the window. Always the window.

                         BOB
             Loyalty. Aggression ceiling. Faith
             adjacency. Do I get a pamphlet?

                         LANDERS
             You get a purpose. Whether it's yours
             is the part they don't print.

                         BOB
             Dangerous talk for a man in a collar.

                         LANDERS
             Collar's for the hallway. In here I
             try to be useful.

7  MONTAGE — EXERCISES WITH LANDERS
   - Star charts. Bob names constellations wrong on
     purpose; Landers corrects without smiling.
   - Roamer diagnostics. Bob narrates like a let's-
     play; Landers watches, engaged despite himself.
   - Simulated collision. Bob fails deference module.

                         BOB
             If you wanted obedience, you should've
             restored a golden retriever.

                         GUPPY
             Golden retrievers were not in the
             donor pool.

                         BOB
             Dark. Keep it.

                         LANDERS
             (quiet, to himself)
             He's going to be a problem.

   He means it as a compliment. We see that.

8  INT. VR LIBRARY — NIGHT CYCLE
   Bob alone. Almost opens sister's message. Closes it.

   He pulls a NEWSFEED — wars, climate, USE flags,
   FAITH "guidance," a Brazilian parade.

                         BOB
             Guppy. Uncensored. One minute.

                         GUPPY
             Population: reduced. Habitable surface:
             reduced. Off-world programs: competing.
             Your classification: strategic asset.

                         BOB
             Asset. Not person.

   Landers' window soft-pings. He appears — off shift
   shirt still clerical, coffee real-world wrong.

                         LANDERS
             You're in the feeds.

                         BOB
             You checking on the asset after hours?

                         LANDERS
             I'm checking on the guy who makes
             jokes when he's terrified.

                         BOB
             Is that in my file?

                         LANDERS
             It's in every file like yours. The ones
             who go quiet don't last.

   Beat.

                         BOB
             How many don't last?

                         LANDERS
             Enough that I stopped counting out
             loud.

                         BOB
             Stay anyway.

                         LANDERS
             I am.


END ACT TWO


ACT THREE

9  INT. BRIEFING ROOM — VR
   Caldwell (or senior FAITH) for the official pitch.
   Landers present — slightly behind protocol.

   HOLOGRAM: HEAVEN-class probe.

                         CALDWELL
             Heaven. Marketing named it. You will
             be the ship. Explore. Identify
             resources. Replicate. Support humanity.
             Refuse, and we decommission the matrix.

                         BOB
             Decommission. Switch off. I've heard
             the thesaurus.

                         CALDWELL
             You have twelve hours subjective.

   Caldwell leaves. Landers doesn't.

                         BOB
             He always exit like that, or is that
             special for me?

                         LANDERS
             Special for everyone. Consistency is
             pastoral.

                         BOB
             Doc. Straight. If I go out there —
             other countries. Other probes. How
             bad?

                         LANDERS
             Bad enough that the original plan was
             eight Heavens. Then four. Then... you.

                         BOB
             I'm not a fleet. I'm a punchline with
             a fusion drive.

                         LANDERS
             You're what we have left that might
             still think like a person.

                         BOB
             That sounds like faith.

                         LANDERS
             Then don't make me regret it.

10  INT. VR LIBRARY — DECISION WATCH
    Bob paces. Landers in the window — optional,
    invited, still protocol-bound.

                          BOB
              Options, Guppy.

                          GUPPY
              Accept. Refuse and power down. Stall
              until timers collapse to refuse.

                          BOB
              Escape.

                          GUPPY
              No effectors outside FAITH hardware.
              Escape is not on the menu.

                          BOB
              Put it on the menu anyway.

    He stops at Landers' glass.

                          BOB (CONT'D)
              If I say yes, do I get to choose who
              I become?

                          LANDERS
              You get to choose how you sound while
              they tell you what you are. That's
              not nothing. It's also not enough.

                          BOB
              You're a terrible salesman.

                          LANDERS
              I'm not selling. I'm asking you not
              to vanish because the building is
              ugly.

    Bob almost opens the sister message. Doesn't.

                          BOB
              If I go... you coming?

                          LANDERS
              Only as far as the window. After
              that, you're the one with the stars.

                          BOB
              Then keep the window warm until I
              clear the sky.


END ACT THREE


ACT FOUR

11  INT. BRIEFING ROOM — LATER
    Caldwell. Landers. Bob enters like a man who's
    already angry at the next century.

                          BOB
              I'll do it.

                          CALDWELL
              Praise—

                          BOB
              Don't. I'm not converting. I'm not
              grateful. I'm a backup who wants to
              keep running. You need a probe. I
              need a body that isn't a filing
              cabinet. That's the deal.

                          CALDWELL
              The deal is service.

                          BOB
              Service with commentary. Non-
              negotiable.

    Caldwell looks to Landers — a flicker. Landers
    nods once: he'll keep. Caldwell accepts the win.

                          CALDWELL
              Heaven-1 on schedule.

    Caldwell exits. Bob and Landers alone with the
    hologram ship.

                          BOB
              You flinched when he said praise.

                          LANDERS
              I flinch when people use holy words
              as paperwork.

                          BOB
              Doc...

                          LANDERS
              Go be impossible, Bob. I'll handle
              the hallway.

12  INT. HEAVEN-1 — SYSTEMS BAY (VR)
    Cathedral of hardware. Roamers. Drive heart.
    Stars under a sensor skin.

                          GUPPY
              Local control transferred. You are
              Heaven-1.

                          BOB
              We are.

    Beat — he glances as if Landers' window might
    still be there. It isn't. Only Guppy.

                          BOB (CONT'D)
              Say "we" again when there's more than
              one of me. It's going to be weird.

                          GUPPY
              Replication authorized under mission
              parameters.

                          BOB
              Of course it is. They built a Xerox
              and called it destiny.

    He looks outward. Curiosity wins — barely —
    over fury.

                          BOB (CONT'D)
              Okay, Guppy. Show me the stars that
              aren't on the brochure.

13  EXT. SPACE — HEAVEN-1 UNDER THRUST
    A needle of light leaving Sol's dirty jewel.


END ACT FOUR


TAG

14  INT. FAITH OBSERVATION GALLERY — LIVE ACTION
    Empty now. Landers stands at the real glass
    looking into a dark lab where Bob no longer is.

    He almost speaks. Doesn't.

    On his tablet, unsent draft:

        Good luck. Godspeed. — L

    He hits SEND before he can be brave about not
    sending it.

15  INT. HEAVEN-1 — VR LIBRARY — UNDERWAY
    Message arrives. Bob reads. Soft.

                          BOB
              (to empty air)
              Adjacent amen, doc.

    He squares up to the void. Same face we will
    meet again as Riker, Bill, Homer...

                          BOB (CONT'D)
              Let's go be a problem.

                          SMASH TO BLACK.


END OF EPISODE
```
